# Institutional Foundation

**Project:** The School of Engineering & Technology  
**Jurisdictional focus:** South Africa  
**Phase:** 0 — Institutional Foundation  
**Version:** 0.2 (working draft)  
**Status:** In progress

## Purpose

This directory contains the Phase 0 founding documents for the proposed independent, privately founded South African educational institution. Read them in the sequence below: establish the recorded decisions, review the D-003 direction and its implementation boundary, then read the remaining open decisions and foundational identity, philosophy and charter documents.

## Recommended reading order

1. **[Decision Register](DECISION_REGISTER.md)** — start here for confirmed decisions D-001 to D-003, remaining open decisions, and change-control rules.
2. **[D-003 Hybrid Model Decision](D-003_HYBRID_MODEL_DECISION.md)** — records the selected hybrid commercial and public-benefit direction and explicitly leaves legal implementation undecided.
3. **[Legal and Operating Model](LEGAL_AND_OPERATING_MODEL.md)** — frames the legal, tax, accounting and education-regulatory review; it selects no entity or operating arrangement.
4. **[Phase 0 Open Decisions](PHASE_0_OPEN_DECISIONS.md)** — records the confirmed outcomes and decisions still requiring explicit resolution.
5. **[Institutional Identity](INSTITUTIONAL_IDENTITY.md)** — defines the proposed institution, its initial and possible future scope, intended learners, and accurate descriptions of its current status.
6. **[Educational Philosophy](EDUCATIONAL_PHILOSOPHY.md)** — sets out the integrated approach of academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research, and independent thinking.
7. **[Founding Charter](FOUNDING_CHARTER.md)** — consolidates the founding intent, principles and institutional limits; it remains preliminary and unratified.

## Current confirmed decisions

- **D-001 — Institutional independence:** The institution is intended to be independently founded and governed, with its own institutional identity, leadership, academic philosophy, and governance arrangements.
- **D-002 — Educational philosophy:** The institution will use an integrated approach combining academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research, and independent thinking.
- **D-003 — Legal and operating model:** The hybrid commercial and public-benefit institutional direction is confirmed; the exact legal implementation remains open.

See the [Decision Register](DECISION_REGISTER.md) and [Phase 0 Open Decisions](PHASE_0_OPEN_DECISIONS.md) for outstanding questions. D-003 does not select a legal entity, implement a hybrid structure, or authorise Phase 1. Phase 0 remains in progress.

## Status and limitations

These documents are working drafts, not a final constitution, legal opinion, registration record, accreditation decision or authority to offer recognised qualifications.

The institution's legal form, registration pathway, qualification strategy, academic governance arrangements and regulatory obligations must be investigated and confirmed before the institution makes legal, accreditation or qualification claims.

## Change control

Keep this document set version-controlled. Record substantive decisions and changes in `DECISION_REGISTER.md`, preserve approved decisions, and update the version and status of affected documents. Mark unresolved decisions clearly; do not silently treat a proposal as settled.
