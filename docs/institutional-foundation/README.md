# Institutional Foundation

**Project:** The School of Engineering & Technology  
**Jurisdictional focus:** South Africa  
**Phase:** 0 — Institutional Foundation  
**Version:** 0.1 (working draft)  
**Status:** In progress

## Purpose

This directory contains the Phase 0 founding documents for the proposed independent, privately founded South African educational institution. Read them in the sequence below: first establish what is decided, then understand the institution's identity and educational approach, and finally review how those elements are brought together in the preliminary charter.

## Recommended reading order

1. **[Decision Register](DECISION_REGISTER.md)** — start here for confirmed decisions D-001 and D-002, open decisions, and change-control rules. Proposals and unresolved matters must not be mistaken for approved policy.
2. **[Institutional Identity](INSTITUTIONAL_IDENTITY.md)** — defines the proposed institution, its initial and possible future scope, intended learners, and accurate descriptions of its current status.
3. **[Educational Philosophy](EDUCATIONAL_PHILOSOPHY.md)** — sets out the selected integrated approach: academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research, and independent thinking; it also lists work still to be designed.
4. **[Founding Charter](FOUNDING_CHARTER.md)** — consolidates the founding intent, independence, educational commitment, principles, regulatory integrity, and matters reserved for further decisions. It remains preliminary and unratified.

## Current confirmed decisions

- **D-001 — Institutional independence:** The institution is intended to be independently founded and governed, with its own institutional identity, leadership, academic philosophy, and governance arrangements.
- **D-002 — Educational philosophy:** The institution will use an integrated approach combining academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research, and independent thinking.

See the [Decision Register](DECISION_REGISTER.md) for decisions that remain open. Phase 0 is still in progress; no later phase is authorised by this document set.

## Status and limitations

These documents are working drafts, not a final constitution, legal opinion, registration record, accreditation decision or authority to offer recognised qualifications.

The institution's legal form, registration pathway, qualification strategy, academic governance arrangements and regulatory obligations must be investigated and confirmed before the institution makes legal, accreditation or qualification claims.

## Change control

Keep this document set version-controlled. Record substantive decisions and changes in `DECISION_REGISTER.md`, preserve approved decisions, and update the version and status of affected documents. Mark unresolved decisions clearly; do not silently treat a proposal as settled.
