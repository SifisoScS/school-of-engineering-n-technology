## 1. Purpose

This document presents the Phase 0 founding direction for formal review by the founder.

Phase 0 establishes the institution's proposed identity, educational philosophy, broad institutional ambition, learner-access direction and intended legal/operating direction. It is a foundation-setting exercise. It does **not** establish that the institution is registered, accredited, authorised to award qualifications, ready to enrol learners, or approved to begin implementation.

The founder should select one of the decision outcomes in Section 6. No outcome is assumed.

## 2. Founding decisions presented for approval

The following five decisions have been recorded during Phase 0. The summaries below are intended to make the proposed direction reviewable in one place. The detailed decision documents and the Decision Register remain the controlling records.

### D-001 — Institutional independence

**Recorded direction:** Independent.

The institution is intended to be an independently founded, privately founded South African educational institution.

This direction does not determine the final legal entity, registration route, ownership or control arrangements, accreditation status, qualification-awarding authority, or statutory classification.

**Reference:** `D-001` entry in `DECISION_REGISTER.md`; `INSTITUTIONAL_IDENTITY.md`; `FOUNDING_CHARTER.md`.

### D-002 — Educational philosophy

**Recorded direction:** Integrated education combining academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research and independent thinking.

The intended learning cycle is:

1. Understand concepts.
2. Investigate questions and problems.
3. Design possible solutions.
4. Build and test.
5. Evaluate results.
6. Communicate and reflect.

This establishes an educational philosophy, not approved curricula, programmes, assessment rules, or qualification specifications.

**Reference:** `EDUCATIONAL_PHILOSOPHY.md`; `ACADEMIC_ARCHITECTURE.md`; `DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

### D-003 — Legal and operating model

**Recorded direction:** Hybrid commercial and public-benefit institution.

The institution's intended direction combines commercial sustainability with a public-benefit purpose.

The exact legal and operating implementation remains unresolved. No single-entity or multiple-entity structure has been selected. No company, trust, non-profit, public-benefit organisation, tax, ownership, governance or inter-entity arrangement is approved by this decision alone.

Qualified South African legal, tax, accounting and education-regulatory advice is required before implementation.

**Reference:** `D-003_HYBRID_MODEL_DECISION.md`; `LEGAL_AND_OPERATING_MODEL.md`; `DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

### D-004 — Learner access model

**Recorded direction:** Layered access.

The intended direction is:

- Foundational learning should be broadly accessible.
- Structured programmes may have transparent, relevant and proportionate entry or progression requirements.
- Advanced or specialist programmes may require prior knowledge, competence or experience.
- Regulated programmes and qualifications must follow applicable statutory, accreditation and professional requirements.

This does not establish that every offering will be free, guarantee admission, remove legitimate prerequisites, or approve detailed admissions, age, recognition-of-prior-learning, bursary or learner-support policies.

**Reference:** `D-004_LEARNER_ACCESS_DECISION.md`; `ACCESSIBILITY_AND_INCLUSION.md`; `DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

### D-005 — Institutional ambition

**Recorded direction:** A broader multidisciplinary education, research and innovation institution.

The initial institutional focus remains the School of Engineering & Technology. A possible future School of Politics & Governance is exploratory and is not approved for launch. Other schools, institutes, departments, programmes, campuses or expansion schedules have not been approved.

Any future expansion requires its own needs assessment, academic proposal, governance review, staffing and capability assessment, regulatory assessment, feasibility work, learner-safety and support considerations, and explicit institutional approval.

**Reference:** `D-005_INSTITUTIONAL_AMBITION_DECISION.md`; `INSTITUTIONAL_MANDATE.md`; `INSTITUTIONAL_EVOLUTION.md`; `DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

## 3. Institutional direction in practical terms

If the founder approves the Phase 0 direction, the institution's founding intent will be recorded as follows:

- **Independence:** an independently founded South African institution.
- **Educational approach:** integrated academic, practical, research and independent-thinking development.
- **Operating direction:** commercial sustainability alongside a public-benefit purpose, with legal implementation still to be designed and advised upon.
- **Access direction:** broad access to foundational learning, with proportionate requirements where appropriate.
- **Long-term ambition:** a multidisciplinary education, research and innovation institution, beginning with the School of Engineering & Technology.

The initial delivery concept is digital-first, with online and offline access considered in future design. A physical campus is a long-term possibility, not an approved project or commitment.

The current repository and landing-page work must not be represented as proof that a functioning school, learning management system, enrolment service, accredited programme or operational campus exists.

## 4. Matters not approved by Phase 0

Even if Phase 0 is approved, the following remain outside its approval:

- A specific legal entity or group structure, including any company, trust, non-profit or public-benefit arrangement.
- Registration, accreditation, statutory recognition or authority to award qualifications.
- Any qualification, curriculum, academic programme, admissions policy or assessment framework.
- Any professional accreditation or recognition.
- Launching enrolment, teaching, learner assessment or credential issuance.
- Establishing a physical campus, workshops, laboratories or other premises.
- Launching a School of Politics & Governance or any additional school, faculty, institute or department.
- Budgets, funding commitments, staffing appointments, procurement or implementation schedules.
- Public claims that the institution is registered, accredited, recognised or authorised where this has not been formally verified.

These matters require separate research, proposals, professional advice where appropriate, and explicit approval.

## 5. Conditions and open matters

The founder should consider the following before approving Phase 0:

1. **Professional advice:** Obtain qualified South African legal, tax, accounting and education-regulatory advice before choosing or implementing the legal and operating structure.
2. **Regulatory pathway:** Establish which registration, accreditation, quality-assurance and professional requirements apply to each intended form of education or qualification before making commitments or public claims.
3. **Institutional capability:** Treat digital delivery, offline access, accessibility, learner support, safeguarding, staffing and infrastructure as design objectives until they are specified, resourced and verified.
4. **Document control:** `DOCUMENT_CONTROL_STANDARD.md` is a proposed convention. Its adoption remains a separate decision unless expressly approved.
5. **Future decisions:** Record material changes and new commitments in the Decision Register. Do not treat an exploratory idea as an approved institutional commitment.

The Phase 0 documents identify issues for further work; they do not replace professional advice or regulatory determinations.
