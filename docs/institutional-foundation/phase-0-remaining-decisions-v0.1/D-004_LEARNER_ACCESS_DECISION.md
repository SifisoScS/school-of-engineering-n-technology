# D-004 — Learner Access Model Decision

**Institution:** The School of Engineering & Technology  
**Decision ID:** D-004  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Direction confirmed for Phase 0 planning

## Decision

**Layered access:** foundational learning should be broadly accessible, while structured programmes may apply transparent, relevant and proportionate entry and progression requirements.

## What this establishes

The institution should distinguish between:

1. **Open foundational learning:** introductory and foundational resources accessible as broadly as reasonably possible.
2. **Structured learning:** organised programmes with published prerequisites, expectations and completion requirements where appropriate.
3. **Advanced or specialist programmes:** entry may require demonstrated prior knowledge, practical competence, experience or other relevant prerequisites.
4. **Regulated programmes and qualifications:** admission, delivery, assessment and award requirements must follow applicable law, registration, accreditation and professional requirements.

Requirements for each programme must be published clearly before enrolment.

## Access principles

Requirements should relate to programme outcomes and level, not create unnecessary barriers. The institution should consider clear prerequisite explanations, preparatory learning, recognition of prior learning where appropriate, accessible foundational resources, reasonable accommodation, varied educational backgrounds, transparent costs, and fair admissions and appeals processes.

## Boundaries

This decision does not guarantee admission to every programme, remove prerequisites needed for safe or effective learning, establish an age or child-admissions policy, create a recognition-of-prior-learning policy, approve qualifications, promise free access to every service, establish bursaries or device subsidies, or override statutory requirements.

## Implementation requirements

Before structured programmes are offered, define and publish the intended learner group and level, entry requirements and evidence, any bridging pathway, selection criteria, fees and material costs, support and accommodation, progression and assessment requirements, and complaint and appeal routes.

## Decision record

**Selected direction:** Layered access model.  
**Foundational learning:** Intended to be broadly accessible.  
**Structured programmes:** Transparent programme-specific requirements.  
**Regulated programmes:** Applicable statutory and accreditation requirements take precedence.  
**Implementation:** Not authorised solely by this decision; detailed policies and programme approvals remain necessary.
