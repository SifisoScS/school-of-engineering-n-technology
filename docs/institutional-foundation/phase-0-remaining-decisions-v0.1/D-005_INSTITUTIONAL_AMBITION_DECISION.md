# D-005 — Institutional Ambition Decision

**Institution:** The School of Engineering & Technology  
**Decision ID:** D-005  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Direction confirmed for long-term planning

## Decision

**A broader multidisciplinary education, research and innovation institution.**

The institution is intended to develop beyond a specialised engineering and technology school over time. Its long-term ambition is to bring multiple schools together under an independent institutional identity, supported by research, applied innovation and public leadership capabilities.

## Founding sequence

The initial school remains the **School of Engineering & Technology**. It is the first academic focus and starting point for digital-first development.

A future **School of Politics & Governance** is included in the long-term vision. It is not authorised to launch by this decision. Its mandate, academic scope, governance, staffing, resources and implementation conditions require separate development and approval.

Other schools or institutes may be considered only through documented needs assessment, feasibility work and institutional approval.

## Long-term capabilities

The institution may over time develop multidisciplinary education, applied and foundational research, engineering and technology development, innovation and prototyping, public leadership and governance education, collaboration with communities and industry, and knowledge resources addressing South African and African priorities.

These are ambitions, not claims that the capabilities currently exist.

## Expansion principles

1. Academic coherence: every school must have a clear purpose and appropriate standards.
2. Institutional integrity: new schools must align with the founding charter and governance framework.
3. Evidence of need: expansion should respond to demonstrated educational or societal needs.
4. Capacity before scale: qualified people, resources, governance and sustainable funding must exist before launch.
5. Regulatory compliance: activities and programmes must meet applicable requirements.
6. Intellectual independence: academic inquiry and evidence-based debate should be protected.
7. Public relevance: contribute to South African development and engage responsibly with wider African contexts.

## Boundaries

This decision does not establish a university or confer university status, approve additional schools, establish a research institute as a legal or operational entity, authorise qualification awards, select a campus or opening date, approve a budget or staffing plan, or grant political affiliation or advocacy status.

## Stage-gated expansion

A proposed additional school requires a documented mandate and needs assessment, academic and programme proposal, governance plan, qualified leadership and educators, regulatory assessment, financial and operational feasibility, learner safety and support arrangements, and explicit approval through the adopted governance process.
