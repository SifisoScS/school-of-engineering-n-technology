## Decision rationale summaries

The following summaries explain the practical intent of each selected direction. They are concise explanatory summaries, not verbatim quotations from the founder.

### D-001 — Institutional independence

**Rationale summary:** An independent institutional identity allows the proposed institution to develop its own educational purpose, academic direction and governance arrangements, subject to applicable South African law and regulation.

### D-002 — Integrated educational philosophy

**Rationale summary:** Combining academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research and independent thinking avoids reducing education to theory alone or to practical activity without sound conceptual understanding.

### D-003 — Hybrid commercial and public-benefit direction

**Rationale summary:** The selected direction seeks to consider financial sustainability alongside a defined public-benefit purpose. The direction does not determine the legal form or tax treatment required to pursue it.

### D-004 — Layered learner access

**Rationale summary:** Broad access to foundational learning is balanced with relevant, transparent and proportionate requirements where structured, advanced, specialist or regulated learning requires them.

### D-005 — Broader institutional ambition

**Rationale summary:** Beginning with a defined Engineering & Technology focus provides an initial institutional direction while retaining the possibility of broader multidisciplinary development if need, capacity, feasibility and approvals support it.

## Decision boundaries

### D-003 — Hybrid model

The hybrid commercial and public-benefit direction is selected. The exact legal form, number of entities, ownership/control arrangements, tax position, public-benefit status and operating agreements have not been selected or approved. Professional South African legal, tax, accounting and education-regulatory assessment is required before implementation.

### D-004 — Layered access

Foundational learning resources are intended to be broadly accessible. Structured programmes may set transparent, relevant and proportionate entry and progression requirements. Regulated programmes and qualifications must follow applicable statutory and accreditation requirements. Detailed admissions, age, fee, bursary and recognition-of-prior-learning policies remain open.

### D-005 — Broader institutional ambition

The long-term direction is a multidisciplinary education, research and innovation institution. The School of Engineering & Technology remains the first school. A future School of Politics & Governance is an identified possibility, not an approved launch. No additional school, research institute, campus, qualification or expansion budget is authorised by D-005.

## Other recorded long-term directions

## Decision control

1. Give each substantive decision a unique ID.
2. Record the decision, a concise rationale summary, its provenance where known, and its status.
3. Distinguish the date a document is reviewed from the date a decision was made.
4. Do not invent a decision date where the source record does not establish one; state that it is not separately recorded.
5. Do not silently rewrite confirmed decisions.
6. If a confirmed decision changes, record the change, its reason, provenance and approval status.
7. Keep implementation details and unresolved matters explicitly marked as open.
8. Update affected foundation documents when a decision is approved.
9. A confirmed directional decision does not, by itself, approve an implementation plan or a later phase.
