# Institutional Foundation

**Project:** The School of Engineering & Technology  
**Jurisdictional focus:** South Africa  
**Document set:** Phase 0 — Institutional Foundation  
**Version:** 0.1 (working draft)

## Purpose

This directory contains the founding documents for the proposed independent, privately founded South African educational institution. It records confirmed decisions and establishes the initial institutional direction without treating unresolved questions as settled.

## Current confirmed decisions

1. **Institutional independence:** The institution is intended to be independently founded and governed, with its own institutional identity, leadership, academic philosophy and governance arrangements.
2. **Educational philosophy:** The institution will use an integrated approach combining academic knowledge, practical project-based learning, research and independent thinking.

## Documents

- `FOUNDING_CHARTER.md` — preliminary statement of purpose, principles and institutional intent.
- `INSTITUTIONAL_IDENTITY.md` — proposed identity, scope and positioning.
- `EDUCATIONAL_PHILOSOPHY.md` — the confirmed integrated educational approach.
- `DECISION_REGISTER.md` — decisions made, matters still open and change control.

## Status and limitations

These documents are working drafts, not a final constitution, legal opinion, registration record, accreditation decision or authority to offer recognised qualifications.

The institution's legal form, registration pathway, qualification strategy, academic governance arrangements and regulatory obligations must be investigated and confirmed before the institution makes legal, accreditation or qualification claims.
